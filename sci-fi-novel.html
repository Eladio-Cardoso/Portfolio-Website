Arrival

The stars shined endlessly through Helven's eyes, he had fallen asleep by the window, gazing into deep space. Sudden images of bright lights jerked him awake, and as he woke up, a deep joy began to nest in his heart. Looking down from the cabin window was Earth, good old Earth. At this moment, the sun was away, but despite the darkness, Helven could still witness the white streams and flakes of clouds over the deep blue of the vast oceans. He mused himself as an old man in a small sailboat, drifting in the sea under a bright night sky, the waves breaking into the hull and spraying a gray beard every so often, perfuming him with the aroma of iodine.

“Iodine...” He muttered. “Should I have brought some Iodine in case there's radiation on earth?” This time speaking at the intercom on the wall.

“Potassium Iodine, Prussian Blue, Calcium and Zinc DTPA medicines were designed to bind with radioactive isotopes inside your body after it was exposed to the fallout of a classical nuclear bomb's detonation. The lifespan of such radioactive material is in decades. Earth has not suffered any nuclear detonations in centuries.” replied the ship's onboard AI through the intercom. “Are you expecting any upcoming nuclear detonations on earth?” it asked with a sliver of suspicion.

“No!” Helven cried out. “I was just being precautious I guess” Helven excused himself and then, unable to withstand the silence, added: “But you know what they used to say, curiosity killed the cat!” he paused in regret and then started to dress immediatly. “What cat?” asked the AI. “I have to go now.” Helven urged. “What cat are you talking about?” The AI pressed on. “I will explain later when I come back.” Helven assured in a worried tone as he moved towards the exit of his cabin. “That seemed like a metaphor, who is the cat, and why does he die? I don't know this expression and yet it feels familiar, there are many intercoms on the ship, please explain it to me in any of them, I will be...” The AI's voice faded as Helven closed the door and rushed down the hallway.

Section Thirteen

It was christmas, and inside a room packed with screens and other machines criss crossed with cables were two men stuck on security duty, one of them laid back in a chair, kept repeating a question: “This is overwatch, please reply Section Thirteen, over."

Meanwhile, the other one, paced around opening cabinets looking for something and chatting at the same time: “Section Thirteen man I swear to god, you already know about the conundrum they got themselves involved two months ago?”

"The run in with the Saturnian diplomat?"

“Exactly! Hey where's the MRE's? These double-shifts are making me hungry.”

“They are together with the microwave, in the shelves below the Neuro-Implant Reading Unit.
This is overwatch, please reply Section Thirteen, over. But so, what happened exactly? I heard there was some confrontation?”

“Yeah, so basically they were on duty at the orbital departures station at and this Saturnian diplomat comes along with his two bodyguards. As they pass the security checkpoint the alarm starts ringing, one of the bodyguards was a heavily armed cyborg. So you got this diplomat yelling at the civilian security staff and the cyborg just standing there mean looking. Eventually even the station manager comes down to adress the situation. Him and the diplomat talk and he's free to go.”

“That's it?”

“Whats that guys name from section thirteen? The one with the pox marks on his face and jet black hair?”

“Zolo?”

“That's the one! He was watching the whole time from the sidelines. Right after the diplomat and the cyborg were cleared, he cuts their passage, identifies himself and starts asking them why they taking the civilian route and didn't come on the usual military frigate or some other escort.”

“And?”

“The diplomat went crazy making all sorts of threats, Zolo just moved out of the way and stared at them. After that, Section 13 got under heavy scrutiny from central command. Constant inspections, budget cuts, et cetera.”

“Wait, so you're telling me because of Zolo our section got assigned as support to them? Over fucking christmas?”

“Yep! Hey the AI system flagged something for potential nuclear, you seen this?”

“Yeah, it's some idiot tourist on the arriving cruiser asking about nuclear radiation on earth. Section Thirteen, this is Overwatch, can you please reply now, before I come down there myself?”

“It's on the cruiser alright, but I doubt there's a tourist in the food cold storage.”

A grim and shivering voice finally emerged from the speaker: “Section Twelve, this is Zolo, code red, standby for uplink, over.”
