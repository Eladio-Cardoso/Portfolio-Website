Sci-Fi Novel

Arrival

The stars shined endlessly through Helven's eyes, he had fallen asleep by the window, gazing into deep space. Sudden images of bright lights jerked him awake, and as he woke up, a deep joy began to nest in his heart. Looking down from the cabin window was Earth, good old Earth. At this moment, the sun was away, but despite the darkness, Helven could still witness the white streams and flakes of clouds over the deep blue of the vast oceans. He mused himself as an old man in a small sailboat, drifting in the sea under a bright night sky, the waves breaking into the hull and spraying a gray beard every so often, perfuming him with the aroma of iodine.

“Iodine...” He muttered. “Should I have brought some Iodine in case there's radiation on earth?” This time speaking at the intercom on the wall.

“Potassium Iodine, Prussian Blue, Calcium and Zinc DTPA medicines were designed to bind with radioactive isotopes inside your body after it was exposed to the fallout of a classical nuclear bomb's detonation. The lifespan of such radioactive material is in decades. Earth has not suffered any nuclear detonations in centuries.” replied the ship's onboard AI through the intercom. “Are you expecting any upcoming nuclear detonations on earth?” it asked with a sliver of suspicion.

“No!” Helven cried out. “I was just being precautious I guess” Helven excused himself and then, unable to withstand the silence, added: “But you know what they used to say, curiosity killed the cat!” he paused in regret and then started to dress immediatly. “What cat?” asked the AI. “I have to go now.” Helven urged. “What cat are you talking about?” The AI pressed on. “I will explain later when I come back.” Helven assured in a worried tone as he moved towards the exit of his cabin. “That seemed like a metaphor, who is the cat, and why does he die? I don't know this expression and yet it feels familiar, there are many intercoms on the ship, please explain it to me in any of them, I will be...” The AI's voice faded as Helven closed the door and rushed down the hallway.
